Technical Bailment & Institutional Occupancy

Bailment within this framework constitutes the formal containerization of digital assets under the immutable parameters of the DB Precept[cite: 1]. By securing the Electronic Settlement Trading Protocol (ESTP) logic, institutions ensure ledger parity, asset isolation, and protection from underlying host-chain vulnerabilities[cite: 1].

I. Architectural Isolation & Logic Access

Institutions securing a Master Seat are granted the right to adapt operational ESTP execution layers to map seamlessly to internal infrastructure[cite: 1]. This right excludes the foundational DB Precepts, which govern the laws of ledger parity and remain permanently immutable[cite: 1]. Public test-net deployment is restricted; all integration must occur within secure, isolated environments[cite: 1].

II. Commercial Framework

Internal Operational Yield

Master Seat occupants retain 100% of operational efficiencies and transactional revenues derived from running internal transactions through the containerized ESTP logic layer[cite: 1].

Downstream Sub-Bailment

Occupants may act as strategic conduits. When licensing the protocol to secondary networks, the Bailee retains 95% of gross revenues, with a fixed 5% origination fee remitted to the Office[cite: 1].

III. Financial Allocation & Activation Parameters

  • Renewal Cycle: Occupancy operates on a strict 90-day rolling cycle, subject to timely clearance of quarterly allocations[cite: 1].
